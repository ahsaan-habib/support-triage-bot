# Account and security

## Resetting your password
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for one
hour. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam and confirm the address with
your workspace admin.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on 2FA under Profile → Security. Flowdesk supports authenticator apps and
hardware security keys. Save the ten recovery codes when you enable it.

## Lost 2FA device
Sign in with one of your recovery codes, then set up 2FA again. If you have no
recovery codes, a workspace admin can reset 2FA for members. Owners without
recovery codes must contact support and verify ownership; support cannot
disable 2FA over chat.

## Deleting an account
Workspace owners can delete the workspace from Settings → Danger zone. Data is
kept for 30 days and then permanently removed.
