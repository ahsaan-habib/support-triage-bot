# Billing and plans

## Plans
Flowdesk has three plans: Starter (free, 1 user, 20 bookings a month), Pro
(€29 per user per month) and Team (€49 per user per month, includes shared
calendars and the API). Annual billing gives two months free.

## Changing plan
You can upgrade at any time from Settings → Billing. Upgrades take effect
immediately and the difference is charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect at
the end of the current billing period.

## Refund policy
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Annual plans can be
refunded in full within 14 days of the initial purchase or the renewal date.
After 14 days annual plans are not refunded, but you can cancel renewal.
Refunds are handled by the billing team, not automatically.

## Failed payments
If a card payment fails we retry three times over seven days and email the
account owner each time. After the third failure the workspace becomes
read-only until the payment method is updated.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available under
Settings → Billing → Invoices. To add a VAT number or change the company name
on future invoices, update Settings → Billing → Company details. Issued
invoices cannot be edited.
