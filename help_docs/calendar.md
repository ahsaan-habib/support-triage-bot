# Calendars and bookings

## Connecting a calendar
Flowdesk connects to Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and any CalDAV calendar
from Settings → Calendars. Busy times from connected calendars block booking
slots automatically.

## Sync delays
Changes made in a connected calendar normally appear within two minutes.
CalDAV calendars are polled every ten minutes.

## Time zones
Booking pages show times in the visitor's time zone. Your own availability is
set in the time zone of your profile; change it under Profile → Region.

## Booking limits
Starter workspaces can accept 20 bookings per calendar month. Bookings over
the limit are rejected with a message asking the visitor to try later.
